Privacy Policy
Last Updated: Aug 11th, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Cerdar Ai Limited ("Airvana," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information in connection with the Airvana MVP website, Games, Campaigns, Accounts, Airvana Points, Airvana Tokens, Campaign Conversion functions, USDT settlement functions, and related services (the "Service").
1. Scope
This Policy applies to KOLs, registered users, Players, and visitors. It should be read with the Terms of Service and Cookie and Tracking Notice. Exchange Partners, wallet providers, blockchain networks, and third-party social platforms process information under their own privacy notices.
2. Personal Information We Collect
We collect only categories reasonably connected to operating the MVP. Depending on how you use the Service, these may include:
(a) account and profile information, such as email address, display name, age or eligibility confirmation, declared country or region, and information used for supported sign-in methods;
(b) KOL onboarding and Campaign information, such as public social-account details, Campaign participation, selected Player Allocation, approved channels, contract and settlement information, and communications with Airvana. The MVP does not obtain social-media account authorization or post on a KOL's behalf;
(c) Game, AIP, AIT, and Campaign information, such as Game events, Campaign identifiers, AIP accrual and use, Campaign Conversion requests, AIT balances and status, attribution results, validation, reversals, and appeals;
(d) wallet and settlement information, such as a wallet or Exchange Partner account address, selected network, settlement amount, transaction identifier, payout status, and information reasonably required for location, eligibility, sanctions, fraud, or payment review. Airvana does not ask for a wallet private key, seed phrase, or Exchange Partner password;
(e) technical and usage information, such as IP address, device and browser information, timestamps, pages or Games viewed, session identifiers, referral information, cookie choices, security logs, and similar information collected through cookies or related technologies;
(f) information from Exchange Partners or other third parties, such as confirmation that a Campaign Action occurred, attribution or referral identifiers, commission or funding status, fraud or reversal status, and other information reasonably necessary to administer a Campaign; and
(g) support, complaint, legal, compliance, and business records, including messages, reports, and information needed to respond to requests or enforce our agreements.
The MVP does not permit users to upload images, video, audio, models, templates, code, or other external files. Airvana does not ask users to disclose wallet private keys, seed phrases, or Exchange Partner passwords.
3. How We Use Personal Information
(a) provide Accounts, Games, Campaigns, AIP and AIT functions, Campaign Conversion, settlement functions, and support;
(b) authenticate users, administer KOL participation, implement Player Allocations, validate Campaign Actions, maintain point and token ledgers, and process or record USDT settlements;
(c) operate attribution, consent management, analytics, fraud prevention, security, sanctions and geographic restrictions, and dispute review;
(d) communicate service, Campaign, expiration, settlement, legal, or security notices;
(e) improve and troubleshoot the MVP using aggregated or de-identified information where reasonably practicable;
(f) comply with law, enforce agreements, respond to lawful requests, and protect rights and safety; and
(g) carry out a merger, financing, reorganization, or similar corporate transaction.
4. How We Share Personal Information
We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. We may share personal information with:
(a) service providers supporting hosting, authentication, security, communications, consent management, analytics, customer support, wallet or transaction administration, sanctions or eligibility screening, and business operations;
(b) Exchange Partners and attribution providers to administer a Campaign, verify Campaign Actions, confirm commissions or funding, prevent fraud, process a settlement, and reconcile Campaign results;
(c) wallet providers, blockchain networks, or related service providers where necessary to carry out a user-requested USDT settlement;
(d) professional advisers, regulators, courts, law enforcement, or other authorities where required or reasonably necessary; and
(e) a successor or transaction participant in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale, or transfer, subject to appropriate safeguards.
Airvana does not obtain social-platform account authorization in the MVP and does not use it to read private social-platform data or publish posts on behalf of a KOL.
5. Cookies and Similar Technologies
Airvana uses cookies, local storage, server logs, and similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, geographic restrictions, AIP and AIT records, user-requested Campaign Conversion, settlement administration, measurement, Campaign attribution, and analytics. The Cookie and Tracking Notice provides more information and describes available choices.
Where required by law, Airvana requests consent before using non-essential analytics, advertising, cross-site tracking, personalization, profiling, pixels, or device-fingerprinting technologies. Rejecting non-essential tracking will not prevent basic Game access or the accrual and use of AIP. A voluntary Campaign Conversion or AIT settlement may, however, require minimum first-party or server-side information needed to validate the user-requested Campaign Action, prevent fraud, and process settlement. Any optional third-party tracking or technology requiring consent will remain disabled unless the user consents.
6. International Transfers
Airvana is operated from the United States and may use service providers in the United States and other countries. Personal information may therefore be processed outside the country where it was collected. Where required by applicable law, Airvana uses appropriate transfer safeguards.
7. Retention
We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, administer Campaigns, AIP, AIT, and settlements, support fraud prevention and security, comply with legal, tax, accounting, sanctions, and contractual obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain appropriate business records. AIP, AIT, Campaign, and USDT transaction records may be retained after an Account closes where needed for settlement, audit, fraud review, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. Retention periods vary by category and purpose. Aggregated or de-identified information may be retained for longer periods.
8. Security
Airvana uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information and settlement records. No service, wallet, blockchain network, or transmission method is completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting Account credentials, wallet private keys, seed phrases, and Exchange Partner credentials and should report suspected compromise promptly. Airvana will never ask a user to provide a private key or seed phrase.
9. Your Choices and Rights
Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also manage non-essential cookie choices. Airvana may verify identity and may retain information where permitted or required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or recordkeeping purposes.
10. Age and Restricted Locations
The Service is not directed to persons under 18. Airvana does not knowingly permit persons under 18 to use Account, Campaign, AIP, AIT, or settlement functions. Airvana also uses location, Account declarations, and related risk signals to restrict access from mainland China and other Restricted Jurisdictions. Eligibility for AIT or USDT settlement may require additional location, residency, identity, sanctions, or Exchange Partner checks. Attempts to bypass restrictions may be logged, reviewed, and result in suspension or denial of settlement.
11. Third-Party Links and Digital Asset Services
Games and Campaign pages may link to an Exchange Partner, wallet, blockchain explorer, or social platform. Airvana does not control the privacy, security, identity verification, custody, deposit, trading, blockchain, or account practices of those third parties. Review their policies before providing information or using a third-party service.
12. Changes to This Policy
Airvana may update this Policy as the MVP, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated version will state a new date and will be posted through the Service or communicated by another reasonable method.
